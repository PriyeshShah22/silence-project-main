System Workflow

The analytical pipeline from raw noise to meaningful silence

Methodology Logic Flow

01

Data Ingestion

Users upload localized complaint datasets in CSV format. The system validates headers and sanitizes data for processing.

02

Density Calculation

The engine computes Complaint Density by normalizing raw complaint counts against regional population data.

03

Baseline Establishment

Statistical means and standard deviations are calculated across all districts to define "Normal reporting behavior."

04

Anomaly Detection

Regions falling significantly below the reporting threshold are flagged as Silent Zones—areas of potential suppression or lack of access.
